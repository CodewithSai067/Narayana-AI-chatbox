Today, NECN stands as one of the leading self-financing engineering institutions in Andhra Pradesh. Located in the educational hub of Nellore—a region renowned for its rich paddy cultivation, strategic coastal advantage near the Bay of Bengal, and excellent connectivity to Chennai and Tirupati—its proximity to major national highways, robust railway links, and industrial corridors provides students with enhanced opportunities for internships, industrial visits, collaborations, and real-time exposure to emerging industries. The campus offers a serene, eco-friendly, and academically enriching atmosphere that supports holistic learning. NECN is graded ‘A’ by the Government of Andhra Pradesh, permanently affiliated to JNTUA, Ananthapuramu, and recognized under UGC 2(f) and 12(B). The college is accredited with an ‘A+’ grade (CGPA 3.41) by NAAC, certified with ISO 9001:2015, and was granted Autonomous status in 2020—empowering it to design an industry-relevant curriculum, introduce modern pedagogical approaches, and continuously upgrade academic standards. Further enhancing its academic excellence, NECN offers a comprehensive range of undergraduate, postgraduate, and research programs, including Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) and its allied specializations—AI, AIML, and AI-DS, M.Tech in CSE, Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE), Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE), Civil Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Master of Computer Applications (MCA), and Master of Business Administration (MBA).Among these, NECN proudly holds NBA accreditation for its flagship undergraduate programs: 💻 CSE, 📡 ECE, ⚡ EEE, reflecting the institution’s commitment to quality, industry relevance, and continuous improvement through Outcome-Based Education (OBE). This reflects the institution’s unwavering commitment to quality, accountability, and continuous improvement through Outcome-Based Education (OBE), ensuring that students are industry-ready, innovative, and equipped for global challenges. Over the past 27 years, NECN has evolved into a temple of learning, shaping thousands of skilled graduates and postgraduates who are excelling in their professional careers across the globe. The college emphasizes strong industry–institute collaborations, a vibrant research culture, innovation, incubation, entrepreneurship development, and encourages students to participate actively in community service, co-curricular, and extracurricular activities. Further enhancing its academic excellence, NECN offers a comprehensive range of undergraduate, postgraduate, and research programs, including Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) and its allied specializations—AI, AIML, and AI-DS, M.Tech in CSE, Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE), Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE), Civil Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Master of Computer Applications (MCA), and Master of Business Administration (MBA).Among these, NECN proudly holds NBA accreditation for its flagship undergraduate programs: 💻 CSE, 📡 ECE, ⚡ EEE, reflecting the institution’s commitment to quality, industry relevance, and continuous improvement through Outcome-Based Education (OBE).
